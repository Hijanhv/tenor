Tenor runs on two networks. **Mainnet** carries the real protocol against real
assets. **Testnet** carries the hardened build (v2) with a seeded market you can
trade in. The app reads every value on screen straight from these contracts.

## Mainnet

Real Ondo USDY splits into PT + YT against real Circle USDC. No mock token is
deployed to mainnet. The market is live but empty — it holds no liquidity until an
audit and real capital are in place, so `fixed_rate` and `pt_price` read 0 for now.

## Testnet (v2, hardened)

The underlying and quote are labeled test tokens, because testnet has no real
assets — but the rates, prices, and balances are read live from the contract, and
the yield keeper and analytics are driven by real Blend and Ondo data from
DeFiLlama. This build carries the safety guards: bounded `sync`, `pause`,
`deposit_cap`, and `set_admin`.
